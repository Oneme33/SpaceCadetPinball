Like the original

  • The table, the rules and every mission of the original, ported from its source.
  • Physics measured against the original game: flippers, bumpers, ramps and kickouts behave the same.
  • Classic pixels or sharp HD graphics, the original sounds and music.
  • On phones: the table fills the screen and the view follows the ball.
  • An easy mode with longer flippers, for a relaxed game.

On Android

  1. Download the app with the button above.
  2. Open the file; allow your browser to install apps when Android asks.
  3. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway: the app does not come from the Play Store.

How to play

  • Flippers: Z and / (or the Shift keys, or the arrow keys). On a phone: the left and right half of the screen.
  • Plunger: hold Space, release to launch; the longer you pull, the harder the shot. On a phone: hold the right half while the ball waits.
  • Bump the table: X, . and ↑ — but bump too much and the table tilts.
  • Menu: Esc or P, or the menu button on a phone. It has a full guide to the missions and scoring.

Missions and ranks

As in the original you are a space cadet working your way up the ranks, from Cadet to Fleet Admiral. Light the mission lights, take a mission at the hyperspace kicker and complete it on the table: hit targets, shoot the ramps, find the wormholes and the black hole. Fill the fuel gauge, build the field multiplier with the drop targets and go for multiball.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. Play in your browser, or install the free Android app.
Does it play like the Windows original?
Yes: the table, the rules and every mission come from the original, and the physics are measured against the original game.
Can I play on my phone in the browser?
Yes. The table fills the screen and the view follows the ball. The Android app also works offline.
Is there an easy mode?
Yes, with longer flippers, a centre post that stays up and kickbacks that never close — for a relaxed game. It keeps its own high scores.
